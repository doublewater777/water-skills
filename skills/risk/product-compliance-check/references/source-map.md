# Product Compliance Source Map

Use current official sources where possible.

## Apple

- App Review Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- App privacy details: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- App Store Review: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/
- Human Interface Guidelines when UI or platform behavior matters: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/

## Google Play

- Developer Policy Center: https://play.google.com/about/developer-content-policy/
- User Data policy: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311
- Payments policy: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10281818
- Data safety: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469
- Families policy: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335

## Privacy and Data

- FTC privacy and security guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security
- California privacy regulator: https://cppa.ca.gov/
- EU GDPR official portal: https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection_en
- UK ICO guidance: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/
- China PIPL reference through official or reputable legal/regulatory sources when applicable.

## Payments and Subscriptions

- Apple subscriptions: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/
- Google Play subscriptions: https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/subscriptions
- FTC negative option rule and subscription guidance when relevant: https://www.ftc.gov/

## AI

- Apple and Google policies for AI/generative content when applicable.
- OpenAI policy docs if OpenAI models are used: https://openai.com/policies/
- EU AI Act official material for EU-facing high-risk AI: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
- FTC AI business guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/02/keep-your-ai-claims-check

## UGC and Content

- Apple UGC rules in App Review Guidelines.
- Google Play UGC policy in Developer Policy Center.
- DMCA / copyright guidance where relevant: https://www.copyright.gov/dmca/

## Sensitive Domains

Check official sources when relevant:

- Finance: SEC, FINRA, CFPB, local financial regulators.
- Medical/health: FDA, HHS, local health regulators.
- Children: COPPA / FTC, Google Play Families, Apple Kids Category.
- Education: FERPA when US student data is relevant.
- Biometrics/location/employment/housing/credit: use official market-specific regulators.

## Search Patterns

Use combinations:

- platform + feature + policy
- app store guideline + feature category
- google play policy + feature category
- privacy law + target market + data type
- subscription disclosure + platform
- AI policy + platform + generated content
- UGC moderation policy + platform
